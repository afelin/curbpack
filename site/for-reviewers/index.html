For reviewers

One screen to decide whether to dig deeper — not a stamp of approval.

Ask the supplier for their buyer one-pager (HTML) and, if needed, the review-pack folder (JSON + markdown layers). A sample of what that page looks like:

Open sample review page

  • Readiness % is a local gate score, not a certification score.
  • Gates passed means deterministic pack rules did not fail — humans still judge annex drafts and risk.
  • Attest / HPURL (hash pointer URL) binds a hash to a commit; UNSIGNED — not cryptographically verified until ssh-agent attest. Unsigned ≠ verified.
  • Not conformity assessment — one-pagers prepare evidence for human review; they are not a stamp of approval.
