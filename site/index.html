What it is / what it is not

Sixty seconds of clarity. Everything else is optional depth.

Is: a command-line tool that checks documentation and dependency rules from data packs (house policy, CRA-style annex drafts, sector packs), then writes review artifacts locally.

Is not: a conformity assessment, CE mark, notified-body opinion, or cloud compliance SaaS. Green gates mean “ready for a human to look,” not “approved.” Not a RISE or agency certification product.

Optional tutor: an airlocked explain-packet may go to Coreward or local chat. Tutors propose only — you must re-check; the packet never greenlights.
